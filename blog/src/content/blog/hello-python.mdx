Every runnable block on this site is a small island: nothing loads until you
press **Run**, and the interpreter (a WebAssembly build of CPython called
[Pyodide](https://pyodide.org)) is fetched once and reused for every block on
the page after that.

Try editing this and hitting run:

<CodeRunner client:visible lang="python" code={`name = "world"
for i in range(3):
    print(f"hello, {name}! ({i})")
`} />

Return values from the last expression get printed too, REPL-style:

<CodeRunner client:visible lang="python" code={`sum(x * x for x in range(10))`} />
